> **Refactoring UI** — Adam Wathan & Steve Schoger

## What this skill deliberately does not contain

- No prose copied from the book.
- None of the book's figures or illustrations.
- No reproduction of the book's layout or design.

The rules are restated in original words as executable guidance. Numeric thresholds appear because a rule cannot be stated without its threshold; several are not original to the book (WCAG contrast ratios, the classical measure range).

## Colors

The book contains no specific color values. Every palette this skill produces is generated by `scripts/generate_palette.py` from the algorithm the book describes. No color was sampled from the book.
